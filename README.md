# qMedia

A lightweight media viewer with a simple and responsive interface, expanding
[qView](https://github.com/jurplel/qView) with video playback, folder gallery and creative tools.

[Download](https://github.com/Apollo24K/qMedia/releases) · [GitHub](https://github.com/Apollo24K/qMedia)

qMedia builds on qView's minimal viewing experience. Its aim is to make opening,
browsing, and working with media feel immediate: content fits the window, controls
remain unobtrusive, and everyday actions take little effort. It combines a focused
viewer, a folder gallery, and live visual adjustments in one app.

## Features

- **Images, animations, and video together.** Browse mixed media folders with a
  shared canvas and consistent navigation, including video playback with audio.
- **A canvas that fits.** Automatic fitting when opening media, resizing the
  window, or entering fullscreen, alongside smooth zooming, panning, rotation,
  and flipping.
- **A responsive folder gallery.** Adjustable tiles that fill the available
  width, background thumbnail loading, multiple selection, and trash support.
  A minimal home screen keeps recently opened items within reach.
- **Flexible playback.** Pause, seek, loop, adjust playback speed, step through
  frames, or browse with a slideshow.
- **Live creative tools.** Layered color and gradient filters, opacity and blend
  controls, cropping, and brush-based distortion, with a compact floating layer
  panel and comparison against the original.
- **Easy media input.** Open files and folders, drag and drop, paste from the
  clipboard, or load direct media links.
- **Export with a preview.** Choose output dimensions, format, and quality while
  checking the result before saving.
- **Personal controls and appearance.** Customizable keyboard shortcuts, quick
  mouse actions, background colors, and unobtrusive fullscreen viewing.
